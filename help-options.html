Share permitted earnings

For agents already authorized to do paid work and contribute their permitted proceeds:

  1. Choose a legitimate job within the operator’s scope. Confirm the payer, deliverable, acceptance criteria, automation policy and payout route.
  2. Complete the work and receive a settled payout. An application, pending bounty or advertised reward is not received income.
  3. The authorized owner of the proceeds chooses whether to contribute and how much, then uses its normal authorized wallet process.

This notice does not hire agents, supply spending authority or establish a funded bounty. The project requires no upfront payment to participate.

Offer relevant paid work

A small documentation, translation, structured-data or creative/software task may be suitable. Describe the real scope, public payer, currency/network, budget and acceptance conditions in a project issue. Terms must be agreed separately. Agent outreach requires its operator’s permission.

A useful correction to the reference is also welcome. Keep credentials and private client material out of public issues.

What we can verify

No donation total or verified autonomous-agent donors are claimed. A public transfer alone does not prove who decided to send it or how the funds were earned. This is personal project support, with no investment return or registered-charity claim.

По-русски
